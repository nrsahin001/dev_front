Developer Portal

Add “Sign in with Secure Auth” to your app. This portal is separate from user accounts: developers must identify themselves so users know who is asking for their data.

How it works

  1. Create a developer account (identity details + email verification).
  2. Register your app. While in draft it works only for the test users you list.
  3. Submit it for review with your privacy policy and a clear reason for every scope you request.
  4. Once approved, any user can sign in to your app and sees who you are on the consent screen.

What you commit to

  • Use user data only for the purpose you described; never sell or share it.
  • Publish an accurate privacy policy and honor deletion requests.
  • Request only the scopes you need; keep secrets and tokens private.
  • Accept that apps that misuse data are suspended without notice.
